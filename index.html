Three directions for the site

Visinage is an independent research, data and visual publication. These are three ways it could look. Every direction has the same three pages with the same placeholder content, so the design is the only thing that changes.

  • Tap a preview to open that page. Its own menu links to the other pages in the same direction.
  • Use your browser's back button to return here.
  • Scroll slowly. Most of the motion is tied to scrolling.
  • Every figure is demo data and the photographs are stand-ins.

Direction 1 · Dark-first

The instrument

Built around one object: a ring of ticks that is the hero, the radar and the logo. Monospaced readouts, rows that open to show the evidence, and on desktop a cursor that carries labels.

About 180 KB per page, plus GSAP for scroll motion

  1. HomepageThe particle ring becomes the lead chart
  2. RadarEight moving topics on one ring
  3. ArticleA pinned chart narrates four claims

Direction 2 · Light, 3D objects

The world of objects

Toon-shaded 3D objects where the object is the chart. Colour bands between sections, figures set as numbered plates, and an article that opens on a chapter object.

About 180 KB per page, plus Three.js for the objects

  1. Homepage3D objects that are the charts
  2. RadarToday's topics and where they surfaced
  3. ArticleA chapter object opens the piece

Direction 3 · Light, colour blocks

The poster

Huge capitals and big blocks of colour. The whole page changes colour as you scroll, photographs are duotone, and the lead photograph dissolves pixel by pixel into the lead chart.

About 120 KB of code per page plus the photographs, plus GSAP for scroll motion

  1. HomepageThe photo dissolves into the chart
  2. RadarEach topic a full-screen poster
  3. ArticleThe dissolve opener and a year ruler
